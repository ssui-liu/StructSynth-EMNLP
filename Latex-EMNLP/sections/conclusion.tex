\section{Conclusion}

We presented \textsf{StructSynth}, a framework built on one core insight: decoupling dependency structure discovery from data generation reduces per-stage sample complexity and enables each stage to leverage complementary signals---LLM semantic priors for structure, discovered topology for faithful synthesis.
Experiments across six real-world datasets show that this decoupled design achieves state-of-the-art downstream utility and privacy preservation while maintaining competitive statistical fidelity, with consistent gains across diverse LLM backends confirming the generalizability of the approach.
More broadly, our results suggest that \emph{explicit structural guidance} is a promising direction for tabular synthesis under data scarcity: rather than scaling model capacity or training data, investing in a reliable dependency blueprint yields outsized returns in generation quality.
