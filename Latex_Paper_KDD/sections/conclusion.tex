\section{Conclusion}

In this work, we introduce \textsf{StructSynth}, a novel two-stage framework that addresses the challenge of high-fidelity tabular data synthesis in low-data regimes by synergizing the generative power of Large Language Models (LLMs) with explicit structural control. Our method first discovers an underlying dependency graph from scarce data and then leverages this graph as a high-fidelity blueprint to steer the LLM's generative process, guaranteeing adherence to feature dependencies by design. Extensive experiments demonstrate that StructSynth consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods in downstream utility while successfully navigating the critical fidelity-privacy trade-off.
